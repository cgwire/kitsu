A brand-new Budget section is now available under the Project menu! 📊
🔹 Track expense distribution and monitor cost evolution over time for your hired crew
🔹 Create multiple budget versions to plan for different scenarios
🔹 Admins can now enter daily rates at the person level, which are used when that person is assigned to a crew role
🔹 For predicted hires, you can use a seniority-based placeholder, and corresponding salary scale
These new tools make it easier than ever to forecast, compare, and manage production costs with accuracy. 🧮 💼 ✅

The Budget section now supports tracking hardware and software costs alongside your personnel expenses.
🔹 Automatically pulls from your Software Licenses and Hardware Items lists
🔹 Helps you build a more accurate and realistic budget
🔹 From the Budget page, click “Count hardware and software” to include these costs in your total
Whether it’s workstation gear 💻 or production software 🎨, your full production spend is now easier to plan and manage.
