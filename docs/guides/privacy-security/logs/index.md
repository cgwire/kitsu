## Login Logs

We've added Login Logs to give administrators better visibility into account access across their organization.
Administrators can now view a history of user logins, including who logged in and when. The log supports filtering by date and person to investigate specific activity, and includes pagination for navigating larger datasets.
Key benefits:
Improve security oversight by tracking account access over time
Audit user activity when reviewing security incidents or compliance requirements
Troubleshoot access issues by confirming whether and when a user successfully logged in
Find information faster with filters that narrow results to specific users or time periods
Scale to larger teams with pagination that keeps login history easy to browse
